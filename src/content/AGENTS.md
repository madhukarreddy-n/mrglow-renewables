- Plain, confident, no hype guarantees (no “25-year structure lifespan” unless a real warranty doc exists).

- Tin shed: adhesive / trapezoidal / standing seam — **final method depends on roof profile and structure**.
- Kits: faster install, less field wiring, cleaner work, fewer connection faults, easier maintenance.
